# Skins

**Fonts (ADR-33):** skins never bundle fonts (app size, and licensing: SF Pro may not ship outside Apple platforms). Every starter theme takes `fontFamily`, defaulting to the platform font; the skin note names the font for the authentic look.

Skins are also **libraries of renderers**: an app on its brand theme can borrow one component's structure from another skin (`MinimalSkin.copy(tabs = CupertinoSkin.tabs)`, ADR-28), which is how "make it behave like Apple's" feedback is handled.

A skin is a **dialect** of Camouflage's design language ([Design Language](/guide/foundations/design-language)): the same components, parameters and tokens, rendered in a particular look and structure. Every skin implements the 33 renderers of [Skin Contract](/guide/architecture/skin-contract), and publishes a dialect alignment table.

| Skin | Dialect | Surface treatment | Feedback | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Minimal Skin](/guide/skins/minimal-skin) | Camouflage's own minimalism | Flat (no shadows, hairlines) | Fade | **v1** |
| [Material Skin](/guide/skins/material-skin) | Material Design 3 | Paper (shadow + tonal tint) | Ripple | Later (reference dialect) |
| [Cupertino Skin](/guide/skins/cupertino-skin) | Apple HIG | Classic or **Liquid Glass** | Opacity | Later |
| [Fluent Skin](/guide/skins/fluent-skin) | Microsoft Fluent 2 | Solid, Acrylic or Mica | Subtle | Later |
| [Carbon Skin](/guide/skins/carbon-skin) | IBM Carbon | Layered (no shadows, square) | Carbon states | Later |

Shipping any Later skin is the author's decision (ADR-22). Each note is complete enough to build from.
