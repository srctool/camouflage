# Camouflage

Camouflage is a component library for **Compose Multiplatform (Kotlin)** and **Flutter (Dart)**. You build a screen once from components such as `CamoButton`, `CamoTextField` and `CamoDataTable`. The look comes from two swappable things:

- a **skin** decides *how* a component is drawn: Material 3, Cupertino, Fluent, Carbon, Minimal, or your own;
- a **theme** decides the *values*: colors, type, shapes, spacing, motion, light or dark.

So a new brand is a new theme, and a new platform feel is a new skin. Your screens do not change.

## Why a skin and a theme?

Most component libraries are one design system. Camouflage keeps **what a component is** (a button with a label, a tone and an appearance) apart from **how it looks**. That gives you things that are otherwise hard:

- **One codebase, a native feel per platform.** Cupertino on iOS and Material on Android from the same screens.
- **White-labelling.** A new customer is a new theme, not a fork.
- **No lock-in.** Write your own skin and keep every screen.
- **Accessibility by construction.** Roles, names, states, 48 dp touch targets, text scaling and right-to-left are in the components, and the testing packages check them for you.
